# Sale scan — 2026-10-03

Read the public product feed of 211 Shopify stores out of 345 brands (130 are on another platform or keep the feed closed, 4 errored). Generated by `node scripts/detect-sales.mjs`.

A brand is listed when at least 3 products and 10% of its catalogue are marked down. The feed shows price cuts only: it has no end date, and it misses sales run through discount codes or banners. Check the brand's site before adding a `sale` entry.

Many stores keep a permanent outlet section or permanent "was" prices, so a markdown is not the same as a sale event. The first group is the closest thing to a real, store-wide sale.
