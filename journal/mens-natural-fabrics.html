Men's natural fabrics

By Dana · 7 October 2026

Another friend was looking for men's clothes in natural fibers: linen, cotton, wool, nothing that feels like plastic in August.

Here's everyone in the index who makes menswear or unisex clothing and works mostly in natural fabrics. Some are small studios that sew everything themselves and some are bigger labels with stores around the country. Unisex brands are in here too, because a lot of the best men's pieces are cut for everyone.

Dana

13 designers

  • Albi

    A Tel Aviv menswear brand founded by Liam Kedem alongside his father, designer Kedem Sasson, born from the tension between a lifetime of craft and a new vision of what men actually want to wear. Quality without the weight of it.

    Store · 144 Herzl St, Tel Aviv
    WebsiteInstagramMap

  • Bein Lebein

    A multidisciplinary label built around hand-screenprinted cotton, heavy-weight natural fabrics (200-400gsm), and small-batch local production.

    Online
    Website

  • Eladan

    Thoughtful everyday pieces made with care and a contemporary hand.

    Studio by appointment · Holit 10, Caesarea
    WebsiteMap

  • Giselle

    Designer Rivi's thoughtfully crafted T-shirt label, locally produced in 100% high-quality cotton, comfortable everyday essentials built to last in Israeli weather.

    Online
    Website

  • Gogou Magog

    Korean influence, local interpretation. Clothes with a clear silhouette and a quiet presence, ones that don't need to shout to be seen.

    Online
    WebsiteInstagram

  • Haptic Path

    Modern, casual-yet-elegant clothing for men and women made from hemp, one of the most eco-friendly natural fibres. Minimalist lines drawn from Japan and far-flung travels, produced ethically in small batches by founder Nina Skibnevsky.

    Online
    Website

  • Kimos

    A modern resort-wear label of handmade, limited-edition kimonos and sets in 100% organic tetra cotton, breathable, thin, and made to move. Unisex, oversized, one-size pieces designed for heat and freedom.

    Online
    WebsiteInstagram

  • Neko

    Born from a love of quality style. Precise pieces in quality fabrics, with flattering cuts and timeless design: 100% linen tees and button-downs, waffle and resort collections, made in Israel.

    Online
    WebsiteInstagram

  • On'nanoko

    ONNANOKO ('girls' in Japanese), from Kibbutz Ma'agan Michael, makes comfortable, soft, natural clothing for women and men. A deep affinity for Japanese aesthetics and a language that moves between the extremes, new and timeless, urban and kibbutz, oversize and fitted.

    Store · Kibbutz Ma'agan Michael (flagship)
    WebsiteMap

  • Rana

    A quiet, precise fashion label offering unisex pieces in minimalist lines, natural, comfortable fabrics, and loose, relaxed cuts. Rana isn't just clothing, it's a way of being.

    Online
    WebsiteInstagram

  • Re'em

    Elemental garments and objects in linen, raffia, and sacred scent, ancient materials shaped into modern form. Silent. Sovereign. Present.

    Online
    WebsiteInstagram

  • Tomer Landau

    Where fashion meets passion and art. A labour of love: flawless designed clothing, hand-sewn piece by piece straight from the studio in only the finest materials like linen and cotton.

    Store · 4 Sderot HaTsanhanim, Ramat Gan and 1 more location
    WebsiteMap

  • Yifat Finkelstein

    A menswear label rooted in ease, structure, and quiet precision. Soft tailoring, natural fabrics, and silhouettes that move with the body, living between streetwear and subtle elegance, shaped by the heat and friction between Tel Aviv and Jerusalem.
